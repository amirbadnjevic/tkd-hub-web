Pavel Khan, head coach of the Uzbekistan national team, took Ulugbek Rashitov to Olympic gold in Tokyo 2020 and did it again in Paris 2024. Same athlete, two consecutive Games – something very few coaches in the world have achieved. Under his leadership Uzbekistan has gone from unknown to one of the strongest national teams in taekwondo.

This is not a demonstration. It is three days with a coach who knows exactly what separates a good athlete from an Olympic champion – and who teaches it.
