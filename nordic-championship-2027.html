Equipment

  • Provided: Daedo Gen 3 electronic body protectors (hogu) and headguards.
  • Bring: Daedo Gen 3 electronic socks, plus WT-approved arm guards, shin guards, gloves, groin guard and dobok.
  • Mouthguard: white or transparent, min. 3 mm, covering the whole upper row (also for minors). Braces guard needs a dentist's certificate.
  • Logama sells protective gear on site.

Weigh-in & withdrawals

  • Complete arrival registration before weigh-in; competitors are called in order.
  • Missing the declared weight = disqualification.
  • Remove athletes from TPSS before 7 January – after that the full fee applies.
  • No refunds after the deadline, except a sole entrant in a category who declines to weigh in (in writing, before weigh-in day).

Freestyle poomsae

Individual, Pair and Mixed Team · Under 17 and Over 17

  • Own music (no lyrics) and choreography – bring it on a USB stick and a phone.
  • Mixed Team: board breaks in spin kick, consecutive kyorugi-style kicks and acrobatic kick are required.
  • Teams bring their own pine boards (300×220×9 mm, max 9 per team).

Rules & format

  • Latest WT Poomsae Competition Rules & Regulations.
  • Single elimination, side-by-side (team categories: regular single elimination). Cut-off may be used in prelims/semis.
  • Recognized: up to 90 s · Freestyle: 90–100 s · 30 s break between poomsae.
  • Poomsae uniform or regular dobok. Poom-grade cadets: red/black collar & poom belt. Colour belts: white collar.

Who can compete

Teams from Denmark, Estonia, Faroe Islands, Finland, Iceland, Latvia, Lithuania, Norway and Sweden are invited. Minimum grade 4th Kup/Gup.

Swedish clubs

  • Member of a club affiliated with the Swedish Taekwondo Union (STU).
  • Correctly completed STU Taekwondo-pass with photo.
  • Valid ID: passport or national ID card. LMA card only accepted for seniors (Kyorugi).

Other countries

  • Member of a club affiliated with the national World Taekwondo (WT) federation – proof of membership at weigh-in / check-in.
  • Valid GAL licence or national federation Taekwondo-pass.
  • Valid ID or passport. Missing documents may lead to disqualification.

Coaches

1 coach per 5 competitors (free entry) and 1 Head of Team per team. Kyorugi coaches must be 16+. Dress code: tracksuit, sneakers, no headgear – checked at athlete check-in.

Insurance & anti-doping

Everyone takes part at their own risk and must have their own insurance. Random doping controls may be carried out by Antidoping Sweden.

FAQ

Quick answers

When is the Nordic Taekwondo Championships 2027?

Kyorugi on Saturday 23 January 2027 and Poomsae on Sunday 24 January 2027. Kyorugi weigh-in is Friday 22 January 17:00–20:00, Poomsae check-in Saturday 23 January 17:00–20:00.

Where is NM27 held?

Kristianstad Arena, Västra Storgatan 69, 291 54 Kristianstad, Sweden. The official hotel, ProfilHotels Grand Kristianstad, is on the same street.

How do I register?

Clubs register their athletes on TPSS (tpss.eu) – find “Nordic Championships 2027 Kyorugi” or “Nordic Championships 2027 Poomsae” in the competition list. Registration and payment deadline: 7 January 2027.

What does it cost?

International clubs pay €60 per athlete (Kyorugi) or per individual/pair/team (Poomsae) plus a €5 transaction fee. Swedish clubs pay 600 SEK. Late fee €25 per athlete after 7 January. Spectators 100 SEK / €10 per day.

Which countries can compete?

Denmark, Estonia, Faroe Islands, Finland, Iceland, Latvia, Lithuania, Norway and Sweden.

What is the minimum grade and age?

4th Kup/Gup and higher in both disciplines. Kyorugi starts at Cadets (12–14); Poomsae athletes must be at least 12. Age is based on birth year.

Will it be livestreamed?

Yes – ReStrike livestreams the competition on YouTube and provides Instant Video Replay for Kyorugi.
